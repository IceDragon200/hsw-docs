# Game Mode - Surface Story - Progression - 100 The Cube

__Arc__ 10

__Tier__ Abyssal-Iron

Abyssal Iron paves the way for better tools, but Steel is the next natural progression.

Unfortunately, its process is cursed once more, the player knows the drill.

## Flow

* Any attempts by the player to prepare steel will instead create "Poisoned Iron" a material that will instantly poison the player if they pick it up
* Nori alerts the player that they have picked up a trail, in other words, they have a lead
* Nori warns the player about "Knight" an entity guarding the reactor facility ruins, Nori describes it as a "thing" rather than his usual tone
