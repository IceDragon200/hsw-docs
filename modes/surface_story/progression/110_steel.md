# Game Mode - Surface Story - Progression - 110 Steel

__Arc__ 11

__Tier__ Steel

Oddities were starting to become the norm in Harmonia, but would that make them oddities or normalities?

Regardless, Steel was now freed from the curse of the Celestial Tower.

With the world growing, a strange Wood Elemental appears and starts causing a disturbance.

## Flow

* The player crafts their first steel ingot
* They receive a message from Nori about the disturbance in the world
* Player can recruit wood golems with mana crystals to do small tasks
