# Lore - Event - Nuclear Waste War

**Era** Emergent

What started as a normal, ill-advised question and answer to a mounting problem, turned into a full blown war: disposal of fissile material.

With humanity solving most of its energy problems through its nuclear programs, it now had a new mounting problem: heaps of fissile material that either failed to meet the requirements for nuclear fuel or had been spent on said programs and now needed a forever home.

The allied nations made ill-advised decisions to outsource the "disposal" of these materials to other "lesser" nations, only for those nations to take the material, further refine it and drop it back on them as nuclear bombs.

While many believed it was intended, some counted it as retaliation for hoarding resources.

With decades of on and off conflict after the first bombs dropped, the Azeros, FROST and Llama Core Tek (often shortened to LLM or just Llama) would rise from the debris as the main power houses.
