# Lore - Organization - Pegasus Forge

**Era** Fracture

Emerging strictly out of a war torn Qarg Republic by former members of the annexed Dalar Kingdom during the Carbi PONA War.

Pegasus Forge was built strictly for advanced PONA construction during the war, with focus on the PONA and Pilot Link Field, despite being an arms company, reports say the engineers and workers were passionate about working with PONAs and treated them more as friends than products.

Outside of its reputation, it's leader Elliot Stone has been labelled as a warmonger and butcher, actual evidence has yet to be seen...

## Theme

Pegasus Forge for its early inception had no theme or identity outside of a name, but later adopted a humble winged-horse for its logo, colors depended on the unit, with "Pack" being its most prominent PONA unit sporting a simple pawprint on their chest often in Pink, White, Blue or Black.

## Impact

Pegasus Forge as with most organizations emerging during the Fracture Era had something of a split reputation, depending on the time and day, Pegasus Forge was seen as a bright neutral forge pushing PONAs out of warfare and into a household name of friends and even family.

In others, Pegasus Forge was a merchant of death and suffering, peddling its war machines to anyone, sometimes even for free.

Its leader Elliot Stone often accused of being a warmonger and outright butcher of his fellow men.

With the deck stacked against them, even if one case had showed otherwise: the abyss truly did see Pegasus Forge as a problem.

Pegasus Forge was considered the most advanced PONA forge producing units that could go toe-to-toe with armies on their own, while not aligned to any specific domain or nation due to existing on a mobile factory or flagship of a similar name: Pegasus, it was hard to impossible to stop them from simply leaving a conflict zone or pursuing them through the vast abyss.

Pegasus Forge became popular for its often heavily armed NAMU units and use of traditional Drones deployed from the PONAs themselves in combat making them an overwhelming autonomous force.
