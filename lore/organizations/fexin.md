# Lore - Organization - FeXiN

**Era(s)** Domain War, Fracture

**Colors** #8338ed

Depending on who you ask, FeXiN is either the public servant who brought many of the conveniences enjoyed by the Wexkyr and its immediate allies to the general public, or the literal Abyss, a machine built and profiteering from the suffering of others.

It should be stated FeXiN of the Domain War Era and that of the Fracture Era are completely different beasts, with the Fracture era's lab turning arms dealer and experts in PONA technology, as well as the sole holders of ASTRA//PONA: the progenitor of the autonomous units.

FeXiN itself was formed by the Wexkyr's former leader Falcon after his departure from the Tri-Race Alliance and was meant to follow his original plan for the Wexkyr: to serve for the public good.

FeXiN itself was split into 2 main departments: FeXiN Labs its primary research wing and FeXiN Materials responsible for acquiring the widely used Kyrstone or Blackstone.

## Theme

FeXiN primarily sports purple as their primary color, accenting it with other colors throughout their history, with the Fracture era choosing purple and gold during its reign.

The choice of purple was simply to get away from the Wexkyr's hard reds which was a side-effect of Kyrstone's appearance.

Falcon assumed purple was "warm" enough to not forget their past, but also forward thinking in terms of their goal, though he'd probably cry to know where yet another one of his projects had gone.

## Impact

Despite FeXiN's bumpy history in the Fracture Era, its Domain War counterpart didn't particularly have any "incidents" so to speak, often aiding Jasmerine Industries with material acquisition and research, FeXiN was leaning heavily on its Lab wing and remained out of the public's sight.

With the discovery of the first PONAs derived from the Rekkaila which emerged from the Dark World at the time, FeXiN's trajectory would split, though it all started as humble research under Falcon's leadership to help the displaced and broken race to reach genetic maturity rather than a "failed" evolution of their race.

With the Fracture, FeXiN doubled-down on their research into the Rekkaila and developed the PONA system through its progenitor, the first PONA and Rekkaila codenamed: Astra.

In order to gather more data about their capabilities, FeXiN along with Jasmerine Industries helped outfit the budding PONAs with Terraforming equipment to recover and build-up remote domains, Astra herself would create agents also named Astra that had the function of producing copies of itself to create a remote workforce in these domains as well as developing personalities and growth for the race as a whole.

For the most part, FeXiN's original lab was not involved in the atrocities committed in Sector Reino by its branch office.

FeXiN's contributions range from high-end weapons, medicine and prosthetics down to the most coveted material in their time: Fexinelle a bio-metal that made up most of the PONA's frames.
