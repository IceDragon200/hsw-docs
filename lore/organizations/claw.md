# Lore - Organization - CLAW

**Era** Paradox

The Scarlet Empire's crowning jewel, saviour and eventually downfall, CLAW was a designed a fully autonomous unit with the only real authority being the emperor himself, a man with failing health.

Once the Emperor was no longer able to command CLAW, it was believed to have run wild, destroying the empire that once commanded it.

However, reports from survivors of the war say otherwise: CLAW was their saviour in an empire that had gone rogue and wild with ambition and power, with the death of the emperor, countless tried to seize his throne of power turning the once prosperous nation upside down.

Had it not been for CLAW stopping the pointless conflict, the world would have seen another war despite being in shambles.

## Theme

CLAW being an AI controlled faction followed through with the Scarlet Empire's will, little was known outside of the primarily blue units looking out of place with the Empire's traditionally red outfit.

CLAW itself had never worn its own emblem or insignia and was primarily stylized in all caps: CLAW.

## Impact

CLAW was the empire's war machine used and sometimes abused to destroy its surrounding enemies to claim land for itself.

It was rightfully feared as a heartless and calculated "monster", at least on the surface.

CLAWs operations became quite erratic towards the end of the war, some believed the spirit of the Empire's old emperor himself had taken control of the rogue AI and turned its weapons upon the corrupt Empire he once ruled, others say the AI finally developed a sense of morality.

Both could be true, neither could be, no one knew the exact details.

As for their impact, CLAW demonstrated the possibility and danger of fully autonomous war machines with fabrication and salvage capabilities.

While CLAW had developed outside of the original PONA project by FeXiN, it is believed that Falcon had borrowed ideas from them.
