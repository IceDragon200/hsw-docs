# Lore - Organization - Jasmerine Industries

**Era(s)** Domain War, Fracture

**Colors** #f9af00, #c20c09, #f9f9f9

Formed by the young couple Torwan Jasmerine and his wife Lancea Jasmerine, maiden name Balfine.

Jasmerine Industries was founded as a civilian-first armoury to prevent a repeat of Wexkyr-turned-Enlightened or abuse of authority that would become rampant after the Enlightened War.

Working closely with FeXiN to train and arm civilians for self-defence, it wasn't without its issues initially, but the Jasmerine Industries name became a home stay.

Diversing their products from general weapons to appliances and even clothing at one point, Jasmerine Industries had remained a people-first business for much of its life.

In the Fracture era with the emergence of the PONA, they had focused on non-combative applications of the war machines, ranging from mining operations to construction, as well as accessories for the small NAMUs.

## Theme

Jasmerine Industries primarily sports red, yellow and white, with red representing Torwan and Yellow for Lancea, the white was chosen as a neutral color upon which both colors rest.

The insignia or logo typically depicted a furred-dragon coiled around golden sword pointing upwards, typically overlayed on a white shield.

As Torwan was a mixed race Red Ogre, Lancea insisted that red be used as the primary representing strength, hard-work and familiarity; in turn Torwan insisted that Lancea' golden yellow armaments be placed at the centre of all depictions, as she was the heart of its message.

## Impact

Jasmerine Industries primarily appears in low to middle-class households as a goto for most needs, while they may appear in upper-class households due to loyal customers, most will take from its sister forge FeXiN.

The organizations biggest contribution is the work on more domestic NAMU applications, primarily for help with child-rearing and health-care.
