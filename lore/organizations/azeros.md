# Lore - Organization - Azeros

**Era** Emergent

The Azeros, medical practitioners turned Private Military Corporation (PMC) due to war and strife in their world.

The Azeros was ahead in every field including blatant corruption, but one couldn't deny their technological prowess, demonstrating that even without the arcane: humanity was more than capable of causing chaos on their own.

During the Nuclear Waste War, the Azeros acted as the primary wall defending the world from the "enemy", while also double-dealing under the table providing arms and equipment to said enemies to prolong the war for their and their allies profits.
