# Lore - Organization - BULWARK

**Era** Paradox

BULWARK was a secondary autonomous unit built by the Scarlet Empire during the Scarlet Ash War, on the surface it was to be the support and utility group to CLAW, but behind the scenes, those in charge feared CLAWs growing power and presence as well as the Emperor's waning health.

BULWARK unfortunately never saw formal deployment at the hands of the Empire, but rather by CLAW itself which activated and seized control of the unit, BULWARK was however tasked with the protection of the remaining lives following the events of the Mana Waves.

## Theme

While CLAW was the sword of the empire swift and brutal, BULWARK was intended to be its shield, shamelessly plated it rich golds, after its capture by CLAW, it was turned bronze to save on cost and one could argue: more tactful.

BULWARK was the face of CLAW's recovery efforts dealing with the refugees and protection efforts during the subsequent mana waves and managed to fulfil its original goal effectively.

## Impact

While BULWARk is not widely deployed and mostly lives on its home domain as the defensive system, refugees and survivors of the Scarlet Ash war sport its bronze tower shield on pins, t-shirts and other paraphernalia.

BULWARK itself excelled at fabrication and recycling efforts which was greatly appreciated during the rebuilding process, their works documented and surviving even the Abyss' wiles.
