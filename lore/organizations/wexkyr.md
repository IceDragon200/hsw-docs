# Lore - Organization - Wexkyr

**Era** Post-Paradox, Domain War

A major faction playing an instrumental role in the survival of the Tri-Race Alliance of the Post-Paradox era, but also the originator of the Enlightened when its leadership had been absent for a brief period.

The Wexkyr were well known for the contribution to the worlds they visited, resolving civil wars or dealing with border disputes, in addition to their generosity in teaching and medical tech.

The Wexkyr owned fleets of airships used to travel between worlds and their primary living space as their home world had become inhospitable by the presence of Kyrstone which had taken over the entire surface.

Subsequently the Wexkyr made extensive use of their natural enemy and resource the Kyrstone, as it was the earliest known MEMS, though had a few stability issues unless refined.

## Impact

The Wexkyr had a long history as guardians, but would face setback after setback until its leader Falcon had given up and founded FeXiN to restart from a clean slate.

Wexkyr's technology permeated every metre of the abyss, from its ever popular spinal implants to its work on spirit-core transplant and effectively eliminating one of the problems with the three-body system making spires transportable across physical bodies without entering the abyss.

To keep them inline originally they were bound to a semi-religious state, but that fell apart after the Dark World War of the Post-Paradox era where only two-fifths (2/5) of the faction survived.

They faced another setback only two decades later when Falcon had gone missing and its High Council had wrestled the reigns from his chosen successor and attempted to reclaim the glory days by drafting and oppressing the local population.

Their plans were thwarted only a year later with the return of Falcon, but the damage had already been done to the Wexkyr's reputation, in a concession, the Wexkyr lost its autonomy and was placed under control of the Tri-Race Alliance for its following decades until Falcon handed over control formally to the Tri-Race Alliance and stepped away.

The Wexkyr had faded from history, but their legacy was still quoted for their impact, though its legacy lives on under FeXiN.
