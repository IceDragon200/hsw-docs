# Lore - Organization - FROST

**Era** Emergent

A former ally of the Azeros, specialized in cryogenic tooling and weapons, as well as their private super soldier program.

FROST was known for its brutality during the war and the frowned upon usage of child soldiers, unfortunately for much of the world, FROSTs war crimes were lighter than those of their enemies and their power sorely needed.

However after the nuclear war reached its climax at the hands of former head of the Azeros, FROST turned its attentions inward with a mountain of battle scared and traumatized child soldiers now returning to their home soil as broken men and women.

FROST would eventually face its collapse at the hand of its own hubris and its AI system RAIP which had gone rogue.

## Theme

FROST was a miserable place to work and live, many were constantly on edge, backstabbing and military corruption were not novel.

Its entire army however unified under the cold, blue, white and black, bringing the crushing winter with them wherever they went.

FROST, like CLAW typically wrote their name on their equipment and reports, sometimes stylizing the "O" as a snowflake in relational documents.

## Impact

FROST had committed grave crimes during its time, including experimental use of drugs, prosthetics and unjust training of minors as well as forced label and blood arenas to keep unstable soldiers pacified between missions.

However, their research into super soldiers as well as the mental limits of the human mind during war time were a treasure trove of data for those willing to peruse it, many look back on it as a warning of how cruel humanity could be in face of a greater threat.
