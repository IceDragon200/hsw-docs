# Device - Heater

Heaters consume some kind of fuel be it:

* [Solid Fuel](/item_classes/solid_fuel.md)
* [Fluid Fuel](/fluid_classes/fluid_fuel.md)
* [Mana](/mechanics/mana.md)

To produce [Heat](/mechanics/heat.md) which is in turn used to power heat-capable machines.

Heaters are intentional more fuel-efficient than devices that directly take fuel to perform work, allowing the same fuel to burn for 4x longer.
