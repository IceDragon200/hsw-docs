# Item Class - Metal Ore

Metal ores come from a variety of blocks, but are typically:

* Stone bearing metal
* Mixed material ores such as Wolframite
* Pure-ish metal ores (iron oxide blocks)

Ore itself is NOT a node, but rather a product of a [Ore Node](ore_node.md), the Metal Ore here is the item form not the node itself and cannot be placed into the world once broken.
